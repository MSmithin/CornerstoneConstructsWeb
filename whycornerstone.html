Why Cornerstone Constructs

What happens behind the scenes shapes your entire experience.

Choosing the right general contractor is about more than price or the finished result. It’s about what happens behind the scenes, the details most homeowners overlook, and how they shape your entire experience. At Cornerstone, we do things differently—and there’s one important factor many homeowners forget to consider that could change everything. We’ll reveal it at the end.

Our Commitment to Safety

As a general contractor, safety is built into every project we take on. We follow applicable Ontario safety requirements and use proper procedures to protect our crew, your home, and your investment. When safety is overlooked, homeowners can face unexpected repair bills, property damage, delays, and potential liability.

Underground Utility Protection

Before excavation, we follow Ontario’s locate requirements through Ontario Dig Authority (formerly Ontario One Call). Damaging buried gas lines, electrical cables, or communication lines can create serious hazards, interrupt essential services, and lead to costly repairs. Depending on the circumstances, liability may also arise.

Landscaping & Property Protection

Improper equipment use, unsafe tree removal, or careless excavation can damage lawns, gardens, fences, driveways, and nearby structures. We take precautions to protect your property and reduce the risk of unexpected restoration costs.

Working at Heights

For roofing and other elevated work, we follow applicable Ontario fall-protection requirements. Unsafe practices can result in serious injuries, project shutdowns, and delays that disrupt your household.

Preventing Costly Surprises

Safety incidents can lead to repair expenses, insurance complications, and disputes over responsibility. We prioritize safe work practices, proper planning, and protecting your property to help prevent avoidable headaches.

When you choose us, safety isn’t an afterthought. It’s part of our commitment to protecting your property, respecting your investment, and delivering a project you can feel confident about from start to finish.

Our Commitment to Innovation

At Cornerstone, we never settle for “that’s the way we’ve always done it.” We believe there’s always room to improve, which is why we stay up to date with the latest building practices, materials, and equipment. By continually looking for smarter, more efficient ways to work, we aim to deliver better results, greater value, and a smoother experience for our clients.

Because innovation isn’t just about doing things differently—it’s about finding better ways to serve you.

The Red Carpet Treatment

It’s the standard we uphold in every home we enter. From the way we prepare and protect your space to how we communicate and carry out our work, every detail matters. We believe you deserve to feel confident and comfortable having us in your home, and here’s how we make that happen:

🏠 Jobsite Protection & Cleanliness

  • Floor runners: Actually laying down red (or blue) protective carpet runners or drop cloths from the front door to the work area.
  • Dust containment: Setting up plastic zip-walls and using HEPA air scrubbers to keep dust out of living spaces.
  • Daily cleanup: Vacuuming, sweeping, and packing away tools every single afternoon so the home remains livable.

💬 Communication & Transparency

  • Daily updates: Sending a quick text, email, or video at the end of the day explaining what was done and what to expect tomorrow.
  • No surprises: Giving advance notice if a crew will be loud, if the water needs to be shut off, or if parking will be blocked.
  • Introductions: Personally introducing every subcontractor or crew member who enters the home.

🤝 Professional Etiquette

  • Punctuality: Arriving exactly when promised, or sending a text immediately if delayed by traffic.
  • Property respect: Never smoking on the property, keeping music at a low volume, and using designated restrooms only with permission.
  • Pet and kid safety: Ensuring gates are kept closed and dangerous tools are safely locked away.

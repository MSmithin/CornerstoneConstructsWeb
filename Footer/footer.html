About Cornerstone

Cornerstone Constructs is North Bay’s trusted choice for roofing, siding, and full home renovations, inside and out. We’re the exclusive local installer of premium Euroshield synthetic rubber roofing, built for Northern Ontario winters. Our exterior services include roofing, siding, soffit & fascia, decks & patios, eavestroughs, and foundations. Interior services cover carpentry, drywall, flooring, insulation, and surface finishes. We also offer landscaping, from hardscaping to softscaping. Proudly serving North Bay and surrounding areas with quality craftsmanship and reliable service. Contact us for a free estimate.
